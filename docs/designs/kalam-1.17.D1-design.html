Rules & plumbing

#Rule
1Alarm times = the DISPLAYED sunrise/sunset of the HOME place (respects your sunrise convention setting) minus the lead; today only, re-armed by the existing day-tick/boot/receiver chain
2Lead presets 0/5/10/15/30/45/60 + Custom (0–180 min); per-event lead (sunrise and sunset independent)
3If today's ring time has already passed when armed → nothing today, arms tomorrow (same as kalam slots)
4Two new request codes (6 sunrise, 7 sunset) beside the six kalam codes; FiredLedger idempotency (code@date) — never double-rings, never silenced by a broken ledger (fails open, as K64)
5Settings fields 43–46: sunriseAlarm, sunriseLead, sunsetAlarm, sunsetLead (blob 43→47, tolerant)
6Polar/no-event days (NoEvent) → no arm, one log line; bell shows greyed with “no sunrise today”
7Viewing other days in the Day pager never changes the alarm — bells reflect and control TODAY's alarm; on other days they're shown but disabled with "alarms are for today"
8Companions: scheduler vectors (lead subtraction, passed-slot skip, NoEvent), Settings codec 43→47, FiredLedger keys for codes 6/7; log line per arm/fire
