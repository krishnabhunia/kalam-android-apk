Clock backup

Keep your clocks when reinstalling Kalam.

Save a backup to Downloads before uninstalling. After reinstalling, choose Restore clocks and select that file.

On supported phones, select “Keep app data” when uninstalling. Android backup may also restore your data, but a saved file is the reliable fallback.

Restore preview

Restore 12 clocks?

This replaces your current clocks and restores their names, locations, aliases, order, expanded views and grouping. Other settings stay as they are.

Implementation: versioned clock-only JSON through Android's document picker, bounded reads and strict validation, atomic DataStore restore, no storage-wide permission. Weather refreshes after restore. No unresolved design decisions; the user explicitly requested retaining clocks.
