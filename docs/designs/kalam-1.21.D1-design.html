Everything marked NEW comes from the Date Bridge screenshots; everything else already exists in Kalam v1.21 and only moves or grows.

⚠ Conflict surfaced before anything is built — the two apps do not agree on the date.
Date Bridge labels its panjika “Traditional panjika · Surya Siddhanta” and shows 22 Sep 2026 = ৪ আশ্বিন ১৪৩৩.
Kalam’s engine (K67) is Lahiri / drik, sankranti-based, golden-gated against drikpanchang (10 Sep 2026 = 24 ভাদ্র verified exactly). Carrying that forward, Kalam would show 22 Sep 2026 ≈ ৫ আশ্বিন — a one-day offset, which is the classic Surya Siddhanta vs drik difference.
This is my arithmetic from the verified anchor, not a fresh engine run — it will be re-verified against drikpanchang at build. Blocker 1 decides which authority Kalam follows. I will not silently switch a drikpanchang-verified engine to another siddhanta.

#Rule
1Six groups: Bengali date & day · English transliteration · Gregorian date · Kalam times & cards (Kalam-only group, keeps the R.K/Y.G/G.K and Sun·Moon text adjustable) · Year & small labels · Calendar numbers
2Sliders are percent scales 90–150% in 5% steps, applied on top of the phone’s own text size
3Live preview updates as you drag; Save changes commits, Reset all returns every group to 100% (asks to confirm)
4Conflict handled: Kalam already stores four absolute sizes (display 20 / header 16 / sub 14 / data 13, Settings fields 25–28). Those become the base and stay editable where they are today; the new percentages multiply them. Nothing you have already set is lost — blocker 3 can instead retire the old screen
5Storage: 6 new Int fields (scales, default 100); tolerant decode, old blobs read as 100%
6Companions: scale-clamp + codec vectors, “phone text size also applies” verified on device, one log line on save
